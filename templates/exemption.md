# Counterfactual-Evidence Exemption

"No mechanism applies" is an escalation, not a waiver. Graduated by class:

| Class | Path |
|---|---|
| T0 | Not applicable — E3 is not required at T0. |
| T1 | Change Owner approves an expiring exemption. |
| T2 | Change Owner approval **+** Verifier recommendation **+** explicit residual-risk record **+** expiry. All four. |
| T3 | **No exemption exists.** Redesign until a mechanism applies, or the change does not ship. |

A hard cap of ≤5 **open** exemptions applies wherever exemptions are available. At the cap, new exemptions are blocked until one retires.
